Patient Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act

At Amazing Smile Dental Care (headed by Dr. Manish Gangwani, BDS), we consider the confidentiality, integrity, and privacy of your dental health information of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal and medical information when using our clinic website and digital appointment systems.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request a consultation, or schedule an appointment, we collect:

  • Direct Contact Details: Full name, WhatsApp phone number, and optional email address.
  • Clinical Appointment Preferences: Selected treatment (e.g. Root Canal, Dental Implants, Clear Aligners, Laser Dentistry), preferred date, time slot, and patient-described symptoms or notes.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized IP hashes (using one-way salted cryptographic digests) for DDoS and brute-force mitigation, browser user-agent, and interaction timestamps.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate healthcare and communication needs:

  • To schedule, confirm, reschedule, or remind you of dental consultations.
  • To communicate critical pre-procedure clinical instructions (e.g., fasting guidelines, medication advisories).
  • To prevent automated spam and malicious abuse of our booking infrastructure.

3. Medical Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We never sell, rent, monetize, or commercialize your personal or health data. Your information is only accessible by Dr. Manish Gangwani and authorized clinical staff directly involved in providing your dental diagnosis and treatment. We do not transmit patient data to third-party ad networks or data brokers.

4. Data Storage and Security Controls

Our website utilizes industry-standard security safeguards:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption for all data in transit.
  • Content Security Policy (CSP), Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), and HTTP-only cookie protections.
  • Atomic file-swapped JSON data stores protected from concurrent write corruption and unauthorized path traversals.

5. Patient Rights

Under applicable healthcare and data privacy laws, you have the right to request access to your recorded appointment data, request corrections, or request deletion of your digital contact record.
