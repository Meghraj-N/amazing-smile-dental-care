Your Safety First

6-Tier Infection Control & Sterilization

Your health and peace of mind are non-negotiable. We adhere to rigorous hospital-grade barrier sterilization protocols for every single patient.

1. Ultrasonic Pre-Cleaning

Every reusable instrument undergoes enzymatic ultrasonic cavitation to dislodge microscopic bio-burden prior to autoclaving.

2. Class-B Vacuum Autoclave

Instruments are sterilized under high-pressure saturated steam at 134°C, destroying 100% of bacterial spores, viruses, and fungi.

3. Sealed Pouches Opened Live

Sterilized tools remain sealed in vacuum-sealed surgical pouches until opened directly in front of you in the operatory.

4. Single-Use Consumables

Syringes, suction tips, gloves, patient drapes, and bibs are strictly 100% disposable and discarded immediately after each visit.

5. Operatory Surface Disinfection

Between appointments, dental chairs, light handles, and surfaces receive medical-grade antimicrobial surface wipes and barrier films.

6. Purified Waterline Filtration

Dental handpiece coolant lines use self-contained distilled water reservoirs to prevent aerosol cross-contamination.
