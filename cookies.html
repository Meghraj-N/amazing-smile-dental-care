Cookie & Storage Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Transparent Healthcare UX

This Cookie Policy explains how Amazing Smile Dental Care uses minimal, privacy-first cookies and browser local storage to provide a fast, secure, and seamless browsing experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text records placed on your browser. Unlike marketing-heavy websites that load dozens of third-party trackers, our website uses only essential functional mechanisms designed to keep your visit private, fast, and responsive.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Essential Security Cookies: Cryptographically signed session tokens (HMAC-SHA256) used for authenticated administrative portal management. These are marked HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure.
  • Client Preference Storage: Browser localStorage used strictly to preserve your UI preferences (e.g., 12-hour vs 24-hour time format preference in the appointment scheduler).
  • Anti-Spam State: Temporary in-memory rate-limiting hashes used to protect the booking system from robotic floods and credential abuse.

3. Third-Party Trackers & Ad Retargeting

We do NOT employ third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or invasive data brokers. Your browsing history on our dental clinic website remains completely private.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Standard browsing and viewing of our dental services will continue to function normally without cookies.
