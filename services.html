Specialty Micro-Endodontics

Painless Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

A root canal is a tooth-saving procedure required when bacteria penetrate into the dental pulp, causing deep throbbing pain, sensitivity to hot/cold, or gum swelling.

At Amazing Smile Dental Care, Dr. Manish Gangwani specializes in Single-Sitting Rotary Root Canals. Utilizing electronic apex locators and flexible nickel-titanium instruments, infected nerve tissues are thoroughly cleaned and hermetically sealed with zero discomfort.

Key Patient Advantages:

  • ✓ Completed in 1 Visit: No repeated injections or temporary dressing dislodging.
  • ✓ Computerized Precision: Apex locators ensure exact millimeter-level canal filling.
  • ✓ Zirconia & Ceramic Crowns: High-strength tooth-colored caps that match your natural bite.

Cutting-Edge Laser Tech

Advanced Diode Laser Dentistry

Laser dentistry represents the pinnacle of modern, minimally invasive dental care. By focusing concentrated light energy, dental lasers vaporize diseased tissues while instantly cauterizing nerve endings and capillaries.

Common Laser Treatments:

  • ✓ Aesthetic Gum Contouring: Correct gummy smiles and balance uneven gumlines.
  • ✓ Aphthous Ulcer Relief: Instant pain reduction and rapid healing of painful mouth ulcers.
  • ✓ Frenectomy: Simple tongue-tie or lip-tie release without surgical scalpels or stitches.
  • ✓ Pocket Sterilization: Eliminates deep bacterial biofilm in periodontal gum pockets.

Permanent Tooth Replacement

Dental Implants & Fixed Teeth

Missing teeth impair your chewing ability, alter your speech, and lead to progressive jawbone shrinkage. Dental implants are the gold standard of permanent tooth replacement, functioning exactly like a natural tooth root.

Dr. Manish Gangwani uses top bio-compatible titanium implants that fuse directly with your jawbone (osseointegration), capped with custom porcelain or zirconia crowns that blend seamlessly with neighboring teeth.
